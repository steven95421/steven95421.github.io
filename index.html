About

I build recommendation systems at TikTok in San Jose, California. I joined TikTok e‑commerce in 2021 as the first algorithm engineer on TikTok Shop’s mall recommendation, built its early ranking systems from scratch, and have since worked on new‑market launches, item cold‑start, and post‑purchase experience modeling.

These days I lead the recommendation work that connects TikTok’s For You feed with TikTok Shop — e‑commerce cards, bubbles, and push. After hours I like building small systems end to end: a board‑game engine, a self‑hosted finance tracker, an e‑paper gadget that lives on my desk.
